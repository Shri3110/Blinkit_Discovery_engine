## Summary of Key Insights for the Discovery Engine:
1. **Trust barrier for high-value/complex items:** Users treat quick commerce as a convenience store, not a trusted retailer for electronics or premium beauty.
2. **Discoverability issues:** The UI caters to "search and checkout" rather than "browse and discover". Non-grocery categories are often hidden.
3. **Intent vs. Impulse:** Users have high intent for grocery items but need highly contextual, well-timed nudges (like the 11 PM diaper notification) to cross into new categories.
4. **Authenticity & Quality concerns:** Fear of receiving open-box items or near-expiry goods limits purchases in beauty, health, and electronics.
