# AI-Powered Product Discovery Engine for Blinkit

## Project Overview

I am working on a Product Management case study for Blinkit. My objective is to build an AI-powered Product Discovery Engine that analyzes large-scale public user feedback and generates actionable product insights.

The engine should help identify why users repeatedly purchase products from the same categories, what prevents them from exploring new categories, and what opportunities exist to encourage cross-category purchases.

The insights generated by this engine will be used to formulate hypotheses, validate product opportunities through user research, and support evidence-based product decisions.

---

## Business Context

Blinkit's strategic goal is to increase the percentage of Monthly Active Customers (MACs) who purchase products from at least one new category every month.

Examples include:

* A grocery buyer starts purchasing pet supplies.
* A snacks buyer starts purchasing personal care products.
* A household essentials buyer begins purchasing baby care products.

To achieve this objective, Blinkit needs a deeper understanding of user shopping behavior, discovery patterns, unmet needs, and the barriers that prevent users from exploring additional categories.

---

## Objectives

Design an AI-powered discovery engine capable of automatically:

* Collecting publicly available user feedback from multiple sources.
* Processing and organizing large volumes of unstructured text.
* Identifying recurring themes and discussion topics.
* Detecting user pain points and unmet needs.
* Understanding shopping behaviors and product discovery patterns.
* Identifying opportunities that encourage cross-category purchases.
* Generating evidence-backed product insights.
* Presenting findings through an intuitive analytics interface.

---

## Data Sources

The discovery engine should analyze publicly available discussions related to Blinkit from sources such as:

* Google Play Store reviews
* Apple App Store reviews
* Reddit discussions
* Public community forums
* Product review websites
* Public blogs
* Publicly accessible social media conversations, where permitted

The architecture should support configurable data sources so that additional platforms can be incorporated in the future.

---

## Key Product Questions

The discovery engine should help answer questions including:

### Shopping Behaviour

* Why do users repeatedly purchase from the same categories?
* What habits influence repeat purchasing?
* Which categories exhibit the strongest repeat behavior?
* How do users currently discover new products?

### Category Discovery

* What prevents users from exploring new categories?
* What information or reassurance do users seek before trying a new category?
* What factors influence experimentation with new products?

### User Frustrations

Identify recurring issues such as:

* Difficulty discovering relevant products
* Ineffective recommendations
* Search and navigation challenges
* Trust and quality concerns
* Pricing sensitivity
* Product availability
* Delivery-related concerns
* Information overload

---

## User Segmentation

The discovery engine should identify meaningful user segments based on observed behaviors and discussions, such as:

* Working professionals
* Families
* Students
* Health-conscious shoppers
* Pet owners
* Frequent buyers
* Occasional buyers

The system should surface which user segments appear more willing to explore new categories and why.

---

## AI-Generated Insights

The engine should generate structured insights that help answer questions such as:

* What themes emerge consistently across user discussions?
* What unmet needs are repeatedly expressed?
* What behavioral patterns influence purchasing decisions?
* Which opportunities could encourage category expansion?
* Which user segments present the highest potential for cross-category growth?

Every insight should be supported by evidence from the analyzed data to ensure transparency and traceability.

---

## Success Criteria

The AI-powered Product Discovery Engine should enable product teams to:

* Analyze large-scale user feedback efficiently.
* Reduce manual effort required to synthesize user insights.
* Discover recurring behavioral patterns and unmet needs.
* Generate evidence-based hypotheses for product improvements.
* Support data-driven prioritization of opportunities.
* Provide a strong research foundation for user interviews and subsequent product development.

---

## Expected Outcome

The final outcome of this project is an AI-powered Product Discovery Engine that transforms unstructured public user feedback into actionable, evidence-backed product insights. These insights will serve as the foundation for primary user research, problem definition, and the design of an AI-native MVP aimed at increasing cross-category purchases on Blinkit.
