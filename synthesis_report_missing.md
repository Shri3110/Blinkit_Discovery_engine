## What prevents users from exploring new categories?

Product Insight Report

- Key Findings: Users are hesitant to explore new categories due to concerns about product authenticity and lack of filtering options. However, well-timed push notifications can encourage exploration.
- Pain Points: Fear of receiving fake products, inability to filter by brand or material, and poor quality of fresh vegetables.
- Product Opportunities: Implementing a 'Brand Authorized' badge, improving filtering options, and enhancing the quality of fresh vegetables could increase user trust and encourage exploration of new categories.
- Recommended Actions: Introduce a 'Brand Authorized' badge for premium products, develop filtering options by brand and material, and review the quality control process for fresh vegetables to address user concerns and improve the overall shopping experience.

## What information do users need before trying a new category?

Product Insight Report

### Key Findings
Users need more information about products, especially when trying new categories. They want to know about authenticity, quality, and details about the products.

### Pain Points
- Lack of detailed product information
- Difficulty in browsing for new products due to poor categorization
- Concerns about product authenticity and quality
- Inability to filter by brand or material for certain products

### Product Opportunities
- Improving product categorization and filtering options
- Providing more detailed product information, including authenticity and quality assurance
- Enhancing the user experience for discovering new products and categories

### Recommended Actions
- Implement better categorization and filtering options for products
- Increase transparency about product sources and quality
- Develop features that facilitate easy discovery of new products and categories, addressing concerns about authenticity and quality.

## Which user segments are more likely to experiment?

Product Insight Report

- Key Findings: Users who have had positive experiences with Blinkit, such as successful orders and new category introductions, may be more likely to experiment.
- Pain Points: Poor quality of fresh produce, unhelpful customer support, and technical issues with the app (e.g., camera feature not working) may deter users from experimenting with new categories.
- Product Opportunities: Introducing new categories, such as the gifting section, can attract users who are looking to try new things.
- Recommended Actions: Improve the quality of fresh produce, enhance customer support, and resolve technical issues to increase user trust and encourage experimentation, particularly targeting users who have shown interest in new categories like the gifting section.
