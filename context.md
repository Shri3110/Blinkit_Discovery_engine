# Project Context: AI-Powered Product Discovery Engine for Blinkit

## 1. Project Overview
The objective is to build an AI-powered Product Discovery Engine for Blinkit. This engine is designed to analyze large-scale, unstructured public user feedback and generate actionable product insights. The primary goal is to understand repeat purchasing behavior, identify barriers to category exploration, and uncover opportunities to drive cross-category purchases.

## 2. Business Context
Blinkit's strategic objective is to increase the percentage of **Monthly Active Customers (MACs) who purchase products from at least one new category every month**.

**Examples of Cross-Category Expansion:**
* A grocery buyer starting to purchase pet supplies.
* A snacks buyer starting to purchase personal care products.
* A household essentials buyer exploring baby care products.

To achieve this, Blinkit needs a deeper understanding of user shopping behavior, discovery patterns, unmet needs, and the friction points that prevent users from exploring new categories.

## 3. Core Objectives
The discovery engine must automatically:
* Collect public user feedback from multiple sources (app stores, forums, social media).
* Process and organize large volumes of unstructured text.
* Identify recurring themes, discussion topics, and user pain points.
* Understand shopping behaviors and product discovery patterns.
* Identify opportunities that encourage cross-category purchases.
* Generate evidence-backed product insights.
* Present findings through an intuitive analytics interface.

## 4. Key Product Questions
The system is built to answer essential questions across three domains:

### Shopping Behaviour
* Why do users repeatedly purchase from the same categories?
* What habits influence repeat purchasing?
* Which categories exhibit the strongest repeat behavior?
* How do users currently discover new products?

### Category Discovery
* What prevents users from exploring new categories?
* What information or reassurance do users seek before trying a new category?
* What factors influence experimentation with new products?

### User Frustrations
* Difficulty discovering relevant products and ineffective recommendations.
* Search and navigation challenges.
* Trust, quality, and pricing sensitivity.
* Product availability and delivery-related concerns.
* Information overload.

## 5. Target User Segments
The discovery engine aims to identify and analyze meaningful user segments, such as:
* Working professionals
* Families
* Students
* Health-conscious shoppers
* Pet owners
* Frequent vs. Occasional buyers

The engine should highlight which of these segments show the highest potential for category exploration.

## 6. Success Criteria & Expected Outcomes
**Success Criteria:**
* Enable efficient analysis of large-scale user feedback.
* Reduce manual synthesis efforts.
* Generate evidence-based hypotheses for product improvements and cross-category growth.
* Provide a solid research foundation for further user interviews and product development.

**Final Outcome:**
An AI-powered engine that transforms raw public feedback into actionable insights. These insights will drive primary user research, help define core problems, and shape the design of an AI-native MVP to increase cross-category purchases on Blinkit.
